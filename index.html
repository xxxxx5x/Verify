How a patient moves through CDMS

Every step connected, so nothing gets typed twice.

  1. Register

    Duplicate patients are caught before they're created.

  2. Pay

    Discounts, VAT and OR numbers are computed for the cashier.

  3. Collect

    Barcode labels, with one tube shared by tests that can share it.

  4. Test

    Values are flagged high, low or critical as they're typed.

  5. Approve

    Medtech verification, then pathologist or head medtech approval.

  6. Release

    A PDF with a QR code, also in the patient's own portal.

Critical values can't slip out

A panic value raises an alert, and the result can't be released until someone records who was notified, how, and that they read it back.

Reports anyone can verify

Every released report and medical certificate carries a QR code. Employers and doctors scan it to confirm it's genuine, which stops fake results.

A history nobody can quietly change

Corrections need a reason and keep the old value. The audit trail is chained, so any tampering shows up when it's checked.

Questions

Frequently asked

Do we need to install anything?

No. CDMS runs in the cloud and works in any modern browser on your existing computers and tablets. You'll need an internet connection. We suggest a backup connection, like pocket Wi-Fi, at the front desk.

Can we move our existing patient records?

Yes. We review your current data and quote the migration before any work starts.

How is patient data protected?

Two-factor login, role-based permissions, encrypted ID numbers, a tamper-evident audit trail, and each clinic's data kept separate at the database level. Employers only ever see what you allow.

Can the head medtech approve results, not only the pathologist?

Yes. Who can verify, approve and release is set per role, and you can choose one-step or two-step verification.

Does it connect to our analyzers?

Not yet out of the box. Results are entered through a fast keyboard-driven screen. Analyzer connections can be quoted as a custom project.

Can we cancel?

Monthly plans can be cancelled with 30 days' notice.
